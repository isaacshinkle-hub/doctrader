About Dr. Ting

Physician by training, trader by habit, diver by choice. This is the trading half of the notebook.

Who's writing

I'm Dr. Ting. I went to medical school in New York City, which is where I first walked past the Stock Exchange on the way to something else and stopped to look. Medicine taught me to work from a checklist, write everything down, and never trust a feeling I couldn't measure. It turns out that's most of trading, too.

DocTrader is my notebook made public: the names I'm watching, a short update before the open every trading day, and the rules I follow when I'd rather not. I manage my own money. I'm not a financial advisor, and nothing here is advice — see the disclaimer below, which I mean every word of.

Why public

It's harder to move a stop when you've written the stop down where other people can see it. A public notebook is a commitment device first and a website second. If it's useful to anyone else, that's a bonus.

What's here

  • Dashboard — my watchlist with live charts, fundamentals, technicals, news, the dates I track, and my notes on each name.
  • Morning Update — the daily note: overnight, levels, on deck, what I'm doing.
  • Tips & Secrets — the playbook.
  • Videos — chart walk-throughs and post-mortems, as they get recorded.
  • DocDiver — the other half of my life: dive trips and adventures, with photos, videos, and stories.

What it isn't

  • Not advice. I'm not a registered investment advisor, broker, or planner. Nothing here is tailored to you.
  • Not a signal service. No alerts, no Discord, no “join to see my entries.” The watchlist is public and free with the reason attached; that's the whole product.
  • Not a course, and not sponsored. Nothing to buy, no affiliate links, no broker referrals. If that ever changes it will say so on the home page.

Common questions

Will you tell me whether to buy something?

No. See the disclaimer. I'm happy to talk about process — writing a plan, sizing, journaling — and that's more useful anyway.

Where do the earnings dates come from?

I enter them by hand from company investor-relations pages and the exchange calendars, and each one has a “verify” link on the dashboard. Companies move dates; check before you rely on one.

What time does the Morning Update post?

Before the 6:30 a.m. Pacific open on trading days. If it's late, the market opened anyway.

Can I quote the updates?

Quote with a link back and that's fine. Please don't strip the disclaimer.
